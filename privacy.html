Privacy

Last updated October 2026

What Minify collects

Nothing. Minify has no servers and receives no personal data. No accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking.

On your device

Your settings (stations, tempo, leave time and so on) and Minify Radio's listening history and starred songs stay on your device, in standard system storage.

Services each app talks to

Minify Radio streams audio directly from each station's own server. Station search sends your search text to the community directory radio-browser.info. When a station sends a song title, that title is sent to Apple's iTunes Search to find artwork and the Apple Music link. To name the song playing, a short audio signature of the stream (never your microphone) goes to Apple's ShazamKit. Starring a song adds it to your Apple Music library through Apple's MusicKit, only after you allow it. Tapping a song title can open it in Apple Music or, if installed, Spotify.

Minify Write sends your text to the AI provider you set up, only when you ask it for help. Your key stays in your Keychain.

Minify Running Late reads your calendar on your device, with your permission, to show your next meeting. It never changes your calendar and nothing leaves your device.

Minify Metronome makes no network requests.

Giving

The "Give to Comic Relief" link opens Comic Relief's website in Safari. Minify receives no money and no payment information.
